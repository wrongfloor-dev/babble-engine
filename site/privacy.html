Privacy

No tracking, no cookies, no accounts. What you make stays in your browser.

What stays on your device

  • Recorded voices are stored in your browser (IndexedDB). They are never uploaded.
  • Scripts and the lines you record for them are stored in your browser (IndexedDB) as well. They are never uploaded.
  • Characters, settings and your language choice are stored in your browser (localStorage).
  • Sound is made on your device. Text you type is not sent anywhere.

To remove all of it, delete your saved voices and characters in the app, or clear the site data in your browser.

Hosting

The site is hosted by Cloudflare. To deliver and protect the site, Cloudflare processes your IP address and basic request data. We don't use this data to track you, and the site loads nothing from other services.
